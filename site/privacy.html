Privacy policy

Effective 26 September 2026

MOVO is an app that helps housing societies in India run their day-to-day work: notices, maintenance bills, meetings, events, parking, services and more. This page explains what personal data MOVO handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

The short version

  • We collect only what a housing society app needs to work.
  • No ads. We never sell or rent your data.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the app.
  • Your phone number and email are hidden from other members unless you choose to show them.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Me → Delete my account.

1. Who we are

MOVO is built and run by an independent developer in India ("we", "us"). For the personal data described here we act as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, together with your housing society's managing committee, which decides what society records to keep in MOVO. Contact: tejas96patil@gmail.com.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure.
  • To show you your society's notices, bills, meetings, events and other features.
  • To let your committee manage the society: approve members, record payments, send notices.
  • To send notifications you need, such as a new notice, a bill due date or a meeting reminder.
  • To send password reset links by email.
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and keep the service running.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account and can withdraw at any time by deleting it, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows, such as complying with law.

4. Who can see it

  • Members of your society see your name, photo, flat and role in the directory. Your phone number and email stay hidden unless you choose to show them. Your committee can change some of these defaults (for example whether vehicle numbers or per-flat payment status are visible).
  • Your society committee sees the member details and records it needs to run the society, including bills and payments of each flat.
  • Market: members of your society see your listings with your name. The other side of an order sees your flat only after the seller accepts it, and the seller's phone only if the seller chose to share it. If your society later joins nearby societies for the market, listings you mark for them show only your name and society, never your flat.
  • People in other societies never see your data, except market listings you choose to share with nearby societies. Each society's data is kept separate.
  • Service providers who help us run MOVO (next section), only for that purpose.
  • Authorities, only when Indian law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your name, phone number, email, photo, password and device tokens are removed straight away and you leave every society.
  • Your market listings are archived and open orders are cancelled when you delete your account.
  • Society records you were part of, such as payments, expenses and meeting minutes, stay with the society because the society needs them for its accounts. They keep an anonymous reference ("Deleted member") instead of your name.
  • Database backups are kept for up to 14 days (daily) and 6 months (monthly), then deleted automatically. Deleted data disappears from backups on that schedule.
  • Server logs are kept for a short time, usually a few days.

7. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as a strong one-way hash. On your phone, the long-lived sign-in token is kept in the secure system keychain. Access to the server is limited to the developer. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • see and correct your data (most of it is in Me in the app),
  • ask for a summary of the data we hold about you,
  • withdraw consent and have your data erased by deleting your account,
  • nominate someone to act for you in case of death or incapacity,
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To delete your account, open the app and go to Me → Delete my account. If you cannot use the app, see how to request deletion by email.

9. Children

MOVO is meant for adult residents and committee members. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app before they apply.
