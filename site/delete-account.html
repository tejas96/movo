Delete your MOVO account

You can delete your account at any time. It takes less than a minute.

In the app

  1. Open MOVO and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

By email

If you cannot sign in, email tejas96patil@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MOVO account". Write from the email address on your account, or include your phone number, society name and flat number. We may ask your society committee to confirm it is you. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your password and all sign-in sessions
  • Your device push tokens
  • Your membership in every society

What is kept

Society records you were part of, such as maintenance payments, expenses and meeting minutes, stay with your society because it needs them for its accounts. They show "Deleted member" instead of your name. Database backups that still contain your details are deleted automatically within 6 months.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
