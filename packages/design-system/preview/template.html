Icons

Iconsax. Outline (linear) for navigation and inactive states. Filled (bold) for active tab, header icons, tiles, and pills. Sizes 24 default, 22 in chips and tabs, 20 in circles, 18 in pills, 16 next to text.

Spacing, radius, elevation

4dp grid. Gutter 20. No borders anywhere. Depth comes from white-on-gray and gray-on-white. Only the floating tab bar and sheets cast a shadow.
